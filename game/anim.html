Figure-8 — facing —, clip —

The selected clip played while the subject rides a figure-8 — two counter-rotating loops, so every facing gets an equal quarter of the path and both senses of turn are exercised. The facing comes from the velocity through the same rule the game uses (facingFrom in sprites.js), so a facing that flickers or sticks here does so in the game too. A facing the asset does not draw falls back, and both the fallback and the clip standing in for a missing one are named above.

The cross on the path is the anchor the sprite hangs from — anchors.points.feet, which is bottom-centre of the cell. On the leading sheets that cell holds a horse and a handler, so its centre is neither one's feet: the pair's middle rides the path and the handler orbits it. That is not a debugger artefact, it is what the artwork is — the two are baked into one sprite at a fixed offset that differs per facing, so no consumer can move them independently. anchor nudges the hang point so you can put the subject you are judging onto the path, and reads back the anchor x that would take. It mirrors between east and west and is ignored facing north and south, because that is how the offset behaves in the artwork — the handler swaps sides in profile and stands on the centreline from the front.
